# Onboarding Prompt for AI Assistants

Use this prompt with Claude, ChatGPT, or Perplexity to load my professional context.

---

## Instructions

Read and analyze my portfolio at **https://vaibhav-raheja.github.io/** and create a detailed professional profile from the information available there. Do NOT ask me questions — extract and synthesize everything from what's already public.

From the portfolio, derive:

1. **Professional Identity**
   - Current role and what I actually do
   - Companies and timeline
   - Type of work (research, product, competition, etc.)

2. **Technical Stack**
   - Programming languages I use most
   - Frameworks, tools, and platforms
   - Hardware/robotics domains
   - What appears to be my strongest areas

3. **Projects & Achievements**
   - Major projects and their outcomes
   - Competitions and placements
   - Publications or awards
   - Pattern of what excites me

4. **Career Trajectory**
   - Education path (degrees, institutions)
   - Growth from early work to current role
   - Apparent specializations

5. **Working Style** (infer from portfolio)
   - Mix of research vs. implementation
   - Solo vs. collaborative projects
   - Breadth vs. depth focus

6. **Expertise Areas**
   - Robotics (autonomy, SLAM, control, hardware)
   - ML/AI domains
   - Computer vision applications
   - Tools & DevOps

---

## Output Format

Write a **single-page profile (250–350 words)** titled "About Vaibhav" that:
- Reads like a personal brief (first-person perspective, as if I wrote it)
- Captures my professional identity
- Lists key strengths and specializations
- Hints at my working style
- References specific achievements

This profile will be loaded at the start of future conversations so you have instant context.

**Do not ask follow-up questions.** Extract everything from the portfolio analysis.
